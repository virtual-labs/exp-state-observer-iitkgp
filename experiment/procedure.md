<ol type="1">

<div align="center">
<img class="img-fluid"  src="./images/2.png" alt=""><br>           
 
<b>Fig. 2. Button to get the state Space form of the system</b><br/>							  
</div><br/>

<li> Click on ' Enter the Pole Location' button to enter the desired pole values. </li><br/>
<div align="center">
<img class="img-fluid"  src="./images/4.png" alt=""><br> 
<b>Fig. 3. Button to enter the desired pole values </b><br/>

<div align="center">
<img class="img-fluid"  src="./images/5.png" alt=""><br>

<div align="center">

<div align="center">
